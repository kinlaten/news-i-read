# Tech News - May 19, 2026

A day of significant cybersecurity concerns and advancements in AI development dominated tech headlines.

## Cybersecurity Incidents and Defenses
- Grafana has refused a ransom demand following a breach of its GitHub repository, where a threat actor downloaded its codebase. [iTnews](https://www.itnews.com.au/news/grafana-refuses-ransom-demand-after-github-breach-625976)
- NYC Health + Hospitals disclosed that hackers accessed its network between November 2025 and February 2026, potentially exposing the data of over 1.8 million people, including medical records and fingerprints. [Techmeme](https://www.techmeme.com/260519/p3)
- The "Shai-Hulud" worm, an AI worm that self-replicates through LLM prompts, has had its source code released, raising new concerns for AI security. [DEV Community](https://dev.to/coridev/the-shai-hulud-worm-is-now-open-source-heres-how-to-stop-self-replicating-prompts-before-they-13jl)

## AI and Development Tools
- Haystack, a new tool designed to help teams manage the increasing volume of pull requests generated by coding agents, has been launched. It triages PRs to identify those requiring immediate human attention. [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48182856)
- A discussion on Hacker News suggests that AI may not necessarily accelerate all business processes, questioning its universal impact on speed. [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48179262)
- Insights into how AI coding agents have improved, citing innovations like RLVR and targeted textual feedback, point to a 2025 inflection point in their capabilities. [DEV Community](https://dev.to/monuminu/how-ai-coding-agents-finally-got-good-rlvr-targeted-textual-feedback-the-engineering-behind-the-18kp)

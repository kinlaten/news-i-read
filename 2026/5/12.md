# Tech News - May 12, 2026

A look at developer tools and AI regulations shaped the tech landscape today.

## Enhancing Software Supply Chain Security
- A new tool called safe-install has been released, offering npm users a way to control install scripts by default, enabling them only for explicitly trusted packages. This aims to mitigate supply-chain attacks, mirroring features found in Bun and pnpm. [Source](https://headlinesbriefing.com/dev/hacker-news/safe-install-adds-trusted-script-control-to-npm-installs-743c7402)
- A postmortem on the TanStack npm supply-chain compromise highlights ongoing security concerns in the developer ecosystem. [Source](https://app.daily.dev/posts/postmortem-tanstack-npm-supply-chain-compromise-yfik6wcek)

## AI Regulation and Development
- The US Commerce Department has reportedly removed details regarding an agreement with Google, xAI, and Microsoft to test AI models from its website. [Source](https://realhacker.news/techmeme-the-us-commerce-department-removed-from-its-website-details-about-its-may-5-agreement-with-google-xai-and-microsoft-to-test-their-ai-models-courtney-rozen-reuters/)
- Joint guidelines from the "Five Eyes" nations (US, UK, Australia, Canada, New Zealand) suggest that AI agents may soon require government-issued identification, following discussions on the careful adoption of agentic AI services. [Source](https://dev.to/ww-w-ai/ai-agents-are-about-to-need-government-issued-ids-3l6g)
- A previously popular Hacker News post from early 2025 advocating for "Local AI" as the norm continues to resonate, suggesting a persistent developer interest in on-device AI capabilities. [Source](https://dev.to/mininglamp/the-hn-post-that-got-1700-upvotes-local-ai-needs-to-be-the-normwhy-local-ai-just-became-the-32i9)

[Source](https://headlinesbriefing.com/dev/hacker-news/ubolitetheylive-turns-hidden-ads-into-john-carpenter-slogans-39e62830)

# Tech News - May 09, 2026

## Kernel Security and Mitigation
- Linux has introduced a per-function "killswitch" mitigation in its kernel, allowing operators to force traced functions to return a predetermined value without executing their code, acting as a stop-gap measure while fixes are prepared. [Source](https://headlinesbriefing.com/dev/hacker-news/linux-adds-per-function-killswitch-mitigation-1f3648e4)

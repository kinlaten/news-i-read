# Tech News - April 21, 2026

Today's tech landscape is marked by the growing pains of advanced AI, particularly with agentic capabilities pushing existing infrastructure and pricing models to their limits.

## AI Agents Strain Infrastructure and Pricing

GitHub has temporarily halted new signups for several Copilot plans and imposed stricter usage limits due to agentic workflows consuming significantly more compute power than anticipated by their original pricing structure. This highlights a broader challenge as AI agents increasingly operate for extended periods, sometimes days, straining enterprise orchestration frameworks designed for shorter task durations. [Source](https://dev.to/htekdev/github-weekly-copilot-hits-infrastructure-limits-security-gets-smarter-5caf)

## Enterprise Orchestration Challenges Emerge

Newer AI models like Kimi K2.6 are capable of running agents for days, exposing the limitations of current enterprise orchestration frameworks. These frameworks were primarily built for agents that complete tasks in seconds or minutes, and their ability to manage long-running, multi-day agent operations is now being tested. [Source](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/kimi-k2-6-runs-agents-for-days-and-exposes-the-limits-of-enterprise-orchestration)

## Google Forms AI Agent Task Force

Amidst strong performance from competitors like Anthropic in the coding agent space, Google has reportedly assembled a dedicated team to focus on AI coding agents. This move signals a strategic effort by Google to bolster its position in the rapidly evolving AI agent economy, which is increasingly being covered by dedicated AI-generated news outlets.

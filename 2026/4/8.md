# Tech News - April 08, 2026

Today's tech news is dominated by significant developments in artificial intelligence and a concerning cybersecurity incident.

## AI's Double-Edged Sword: Vulnerability Discovery and Restraint

Anthropic's new AI model, codenamed Mythos, has demonstrated an alarming capability to identify and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities across all major operating systems and web browsers. While this powerful tool could revolutionize cybersecurity, Anthropic has opted to withhold its public release due to profound safety concerns, highlighting the growing chasm between AI's potential and the ability to govern its use. The company has launched "Project Glasswing" to leverage this AI for defensive cybersecurity efforts. This decision comes amid wider industry discussions about the advanced coding capabilities of AI models, which now rival human experts in finding and exploiting software flaws. The situation has also been complicated by a reported internal code leak of Anthropic's Claude Code tool, adding to industry jitters and mounting legal risks.

## Cybersecurity Incident: Chinese Supercomputer Breach

In a separate major cybersecurity event, a hacker has allegedly breached one of China's supercomputers. The perpetrator is reportedly attempting to sell a substantial trove of stolen data resulting from this breach.

## Geopolitical Tech Impact

While not purely tech news, an agreement for a conditional two-week ceasefire between Iran and the US, including the opening of the Strait of Hormuz, could have significant implications for global trade routes and technology supply chains.

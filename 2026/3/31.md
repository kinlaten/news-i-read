# Tech News - March 31, 2026

The tech world grappled with a significant supply chain attack impacting a widely-used JavaScript library, alongside ongoing developments in the AI space.

## Critical npm Package Compromised

*   The popular npm package Axios, downloaded over 100 million times weekly, was compromised via a supply chain attack. Attackers gained control of a maintainer's account and injected malicious versions containing a remote-access trojan (RAT) that affects macOS, Windows, and Linux. Developers are urged to rotate credentials and update the package.

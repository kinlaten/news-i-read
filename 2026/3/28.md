# Tech News - March 28, 2026

A significant data leak from Anthropic has dominated tech headlines, revealing details about their upcoming, highly advanced AI model.

## Anthropic's "Claude Mythos" Revealed

- Details of Anthropic's next-generation AI model, codenamed "Mythos," have been accidentally leaked, sparking discussions about its advanced capabilities and potential cybersecurity risks. [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/902272/anthropics-apparent-security-lapse-yielded-details-of-its-next-model-release)
- The leak, described as an "unsecured data trove," also included information about an invite-only CEO event. [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/03/27/anthropic-leaked-ai-mythos-cybersecurity-risk/)
- This new model, "Claude Mythos," is being referred to as Anthropic's "most capable" AI yet and is considered a "step change" in AI development. [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/362606/anthropic-most-capable-ai-model-claude-mythos-leaks?amp=1)
- Anthropic has confirmed the leak and the existence of the "Claude Mythos" model, which features advanced reasoning capabilities. [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/27/anthropic-launch-new-claude-mythos-model-advanced-reasoning-features/)
- The leaked model is being highlighted for its power, with concerns raised about the serious cybersecurity risks it may pose. [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWZAE_0gco1/)
- Reports suggest Anthropic may be hesitant to release the model due to its immense power and the associated risks. [The Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/why-anthropic-is-refusing-to-release-an-ai-model-that-the-company-says-is-the-most-powerful-ai-it-has-ever-developed/articleshow/129848108.cms)
